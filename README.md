# B2 Unisex Salon — Website Concept

A premium, mobile-first website concept created for **B2 Unisex Salon, Ibadan, Nigeria**.

The concept demonstrates how a modern salon could present its brand, services, location and customer experience through a professional digital presence.

> **Note:** This is a demonstration concept and is not the official website of B2 Unisex Salon. Publicly available business information was used where appropriate, while unverified information, prices, awards and claims were intentionally excluded.

---

## ✨ Concept Highlights

- Premium dark luxury salon aesthetic
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Elegant typography
- Smooth scroll animations
- Scroll-reveal effects
- Animated marquee section
- Interactive service cards
- Editorial-style image gallery
- Appointment call-to-action
- WhatsApp contact integration
- Direct phone-call button
- Google Maps directions
- Mobile sticky contact bar
- Responsive navigation menu
- Subtle grain/noise visual effect
- Desktop and Android-friendly layouts

---

## 📱 Mobile Experience

The website was designed mobile-first because many potential salon customers will discover and contact the business from their phones.

The mobile experience includes:

- Large readable typography
- Touch-friendly buttons
- Horizontal swipe gallery
- Sticky Call / WhatsApp actions
- Compact navigation menu
- Responsive images
- Optimized spacing
- Smooth animations

---

## 🖥️ Desktop Experience

On larger screens, the website expands into an editorial-style layout with:

- Large hero composition
- Premium typography
- Multi-column service layout
- Asymmetrical gallery
- Floating visual elements
- Spacious sections
- Large CTA moments

## ⚠️ Information Policy

This concept intentionally avoids fabricating business information.

The following should be confirmed directly with the salon before launching a production website:

- Exact service list
- Service descriptions
- Prices
- Opening hours
- Official business biography
- Staff information
- Official social media accounts
- Salon photographs
- Booking procedures
- Promotions
- Additional customer testimonials

Concept images should also be replaced with photographs supplied or approved by the salon.

## 🎨 Design Direction

The visual identity uses:

**Primary aesthetic:**  
Dark luxury / editorial beauty

**Typography:**  
Playfair Display + DM Sans

**Visual direction:**  
Elegant, sophisticated, modern and minimal.

The goal is to make the website feel like a premium Nigerian beauty brand rather than a generic website template.
